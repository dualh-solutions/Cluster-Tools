## Extract Specific Pages from Any PDF — Privately

Sometimes a 50-page PDF report contains only 3 pages you actually need. The Cluster Tools PDF Page Extractor lets you select any pages from a PDF and save them as a new, standalone PDF — without the rest.

This runs entirely in your browser using [PDF-lib](https://pdf-lib.js.org/). Your document never leaves your device. This is especially important for confidential PDFs — contracts, financial reports, medical records — where uploading to a cloud service creates unnecessary risk.

## When to Extract PDF Pages

**Sharing relevant sections** — rather than emailing a 100-page handbook, extract the 5 relevant pages and send those.

**Building custom document packages** — select pages 1, 5, 12, and 45 from a report to create a tailored summary.

**Separating a scanned multi-document PDF** — if a scanner combined multiple documents into one PDF, extract each document's pages into separate files.

**Reducing file size** — sometimes the most effective compression is simply removing pages you don't need.

## Step-by-Step: How to Extract Pages

1. **Upload your PDF** — drag and drop it into the tool.
2. **Preview the pages** — thumbnails of all pages are displayed.
3. **Select the pages you want** — click individual page thumbnails, or enter a page range (e.g., `1-5, 10, 15-20`).
4. **Click "Extract"** — the selected pages are assembled into a new PDF in your browser.
5. **Download** the extracted PDF.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does extraction preserve the original PDF formatting?**
Yes — PDF-lib copies the full page content (text, images, fonts, vector graphics) from the source document into the new PDF. The extracted pages look identical to the originals.

**Can I extract non-consecutive pages?**
Yes. Select individual pages by clicking them, or enter a range like `3, 7, 12-15` to extract pages 3, 7, and 12 through 15.

**What happens to password-protected PDFs?**
Encrypted PDFs must be unlocked before processing. If the PDF requires a password to open, you'll need to supply it.

**Is there a limit on the number of pages I can extract?**
No. You can extract any number of pages from any size PDF, limited only by browser memory.
