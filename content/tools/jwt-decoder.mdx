## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Does this verify the JWT signature?**
No. This tool only decodes the header and payload for inspection. Signature verification requires the secret or public key, which must be done server-side.

**Is my JWT sent to a server?**
No. All decoding happens in your browser using base64url decoding and JSON.parse. Nothing leaves your device.
