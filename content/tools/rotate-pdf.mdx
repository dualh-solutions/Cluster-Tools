## Rotate PDF Pages — Fix Orientation Without a Server

Scanned documents often come out sideways. PDFs from phones may have the wrong orientation. The Cluster Tools PDF Rotator lets you rotate any or all pages in a PDF by 90°, 180°, or 270° — and save the corrected document.

All processing uses [PDF-lib](https://pdf-lib.js.org/) in your browser. Your PDF never goes to a server.

## Step-by-Step: How to Rotate Pages

1. **Drop your PDF** into the tool above.
2. **View page thumbnails** — current orientation of each page is visible.
3. **Select pages to rotate** — click individual pages or select all.
4. **Choose rotation** — 90° clockwise, 90° counterclockwise (270° clockwise), or 180°.
5. **Apply and download** the rotated PDF.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does rotating affect image quality?**
No. PDF rotation is metadata-level — it changes the viewing angle specified in the page dictionary without re-encoding any content. There's no quality loss.

**Can I rotate only some pages and not others?**
Yes — select individual pages by clicking their thumbnails. Only selected pages are rotated.

**Does this work on password-protected PDFs?**
Only if you supply the correct password to unlock it first.
