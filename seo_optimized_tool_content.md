### Implementation Notes for Phase 4 & 5 Compliance:
1. **No Keyword Stuffing:** The phrasing uses natural variations like "extract audio," "offline playback," "audio fidelity," "cover image," and "click-through rate (CTR)" instead of repeating the main keyword blindly.
2. **Entities Addressed:** Bitrate (320kbps), file types (MP3, JPG), devices/browsers (iOS, Safari), and industry concepts (video SEO, CTR) are naturally woven in to establish topical authority.
3. **Semantic HTML Elements:** The structure uses `<main>`, `<header>`, `<section>`, `<article>`, and logical `<h1/h2/h3>` tags.
4. **FAQ Schema:** The FAQ sections are perfectly formatted to be parsed into JSON-LD `FAQPage` schema on the frontend. 
5. **Human Experience:** Provides genuine insight ("When you're commuting through areas with spotty cellular coverage...", "examining high-performing thumbnails in full detail allows you to study typography...").
